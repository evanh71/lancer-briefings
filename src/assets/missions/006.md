## Break Their Bows

# Goals

- Infiltrate the blacksite controlling the identified missile array, gathering whatever information you can.
- Render the missile silos unusable, whether through force or subterfuge.

# Stakes

- The ability for the Last Standard to launch further large scale attacks
- Enable revolutionaries by reducing threats of violence.

---

> After analyzing the coordinates embedded in Tuazon's message, a series of missile silos have been identified, hidden in Ark's lithosphere. These are not the only silos responsible for the Thousand Arrow Protocol, but destroying these would no doubt leave a dent in the Last Standard's arsenal. This is also a rare opportunity to identify and explore one of the various blacksites on Ark. Almost certainly, a myriad of secrets lie here if you're willing to risk a stealthier approach.
