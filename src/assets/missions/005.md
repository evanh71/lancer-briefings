> We have reason to believe that a weather control array is buried somewhere under the ruins of Terminal Ivon. If we can secure the technology then we may be able to influence the weather in our favour. The terminal has been abandoned for a long time however, reports suggest seismic aftershocks are still occuring to this day. Be prepared for an environment that has learned to live without human life.
